About PBS

PBS is a developing transport and trading information platform based in Lamjung, Besishahar, Nepal.


The website was created by Sworup Poudel, a young creator from Lamjung, Besishahar, with the goal of providing useful information about transportation, vehicle rental and trading.


PBS focuses on information about trucks, JCBs, tractors, buses and transportation facilities for construction, mining and goods transportation.

What is a Candlestick?

A candlestick shows price movement during a particular period. It contains an open price, close price, high price and low price.

Common Candlestick Patterns

Doji • Hammer • Inverted Hammer • Shooting Star • Engulfing • Morning Star • Evening Star • Three White Soldiers • Three Black Crows
